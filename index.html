THE RESEARCH QUESTION

Can short, repeatable fingertip PPG recordings capture useful changes in an individual's peripheral pulse waveform?

VOCALERT is a measurement and data-collection prototype. It starts with signal acquisition, technical quality, and repeatability. The future sickle-cell research hypothesis requires a separate, ethically approved longitudinal study.

Research boundary

The 2026 sickle-cell PPG study supports feasibility of monitoring vasoreactivity with wearable PPG; it does not validate 30-second smartphone readings as a predictor of an imminent vaso-occlusive crisis. VOCALERT does not calculate a crisis probability.

1
Warm your hands

If your hands are cold, wait until they are comfortably warm. Do not use extreme heat.

2
Cover both parts

Place the soft pad of your index finger over the rear camera lens and adjacent flash.

3
Use light pressure

Keep contact steady without squeezing. Excess pressure can alter local blood flow.

4
Stay still

Sit quietly, support your hand, and avoid talking or moving during the capture.

BEFORE YOU START

Capture checklist

  • Phone is on a stable surface
  • Camera lens and flash are clean
  • Finger pad covers lens and flash
  • Pressure is light and steady
  • Hand is comfortably warm
  • No talking or movement

Research notes & protocol

The engineering prototype is only the first stage. The clinical hypothesis needs its own evidence.

∿
SCIENTIFIC HYPOTHESIS — NOT AN ESTABLISHED CLINICAL CLAIM

Do personalized peripheral PPG changes contain information about subsequent pain events in people with sickle-cell disease?

A small 2026 feasibility study explored overnight PPG vasoreactivity markers in 12 people with SCD and 6 controls. This app does not reproduce that protocol or validate its markers.

01 / ENGINEERING

Validate the phone signal

Compare camera-derived pulse rate and waveform quality with a reference device under controlled conditions. Characterize phone model, frame rate, finger pressure, temperature, motion, and skin-tone-related measurement differences.

  • Repeated recordings per participant
  • Agreement analysis, not correlation alone
  • Bland–Altman limits and MAE
  • Signal failure and abstention rates
02 / PHYSIOLOGY

Characterize repeatability

Determine whether relative pulse amplitude and beat-to-beat amplitude variability are repeatable across sessions. Do not call a short-spot-check feature “Mvasoc” or equate it with the overnight PPGampCV used in the cited SCD study.

  • Within-person repeatability
  • Day-to-day variation
  • Cold hand / pressure / position effects
  • Device-to-device variation
03 / CLINICAL

Prospective SCD validation

Only after engineering validation, collaborate with a hematology service to collect longitudinal reference-grade PPG and accurately timestamped pain outcomes. This requires ethics approval and clinical oversight.

  • Pre-register the prediction horizon
  • Participant-level validation splits
  • Time-dependent sensitivity and calibration
  • External validation at another site
What VOCALERT does not do

It does not screen for sickle-cell disease, measure blood oxygen, measure blood pressure, diagnose vasoconstriction, predict a VOC, or tell someone to seek or avoid care. The camera-derived pulse estimate is not a replacement for an ECG or medical pulse oximeter.
